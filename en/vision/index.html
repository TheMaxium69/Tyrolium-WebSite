Vision

Our Conquest

Building the tech giant France never managed to build. Independent, sovereign, and driven by a generation with no intention of waiting its turn.

The observation

Tech is the oil of our generation.

While "made in France" gets celebrated at every trade show, our national health data sits on Microsoft servers, our start-ups sell out to the Americans at the first cheque, and our champions wear borrowed names. We have normalised dependency. Worse: we call it ambition.

Facing the United States and China, artificial intelligence widens the gap every month. Technology is no longer one sector among others: it is the energy, the defence and the influence of the 21st century. A country that does not control its tech no longer controls anything.

Tyrolium was born from that refusal. We do not see other nations as enemies, but as competitors. And competitors are there to be beaten - with better products, better prices and more nerve.

Technology is not a rich kid's hobby. It is the oil, the nuclear bomb of my generation.

Maxime Tournier

Where we come from

Born in a teenager's bedroom. Forged in conflict.

It all starts in 2017. Maxime Tournier is 13, with a keyboard and one obsession: building things himself. The first project is a Minecraft server. It would turn out to be the toughest school there is.

Dozens of conflicts, lawsuits, stolen property, takeover attempts by former members - hacks and database breaches included. Where others would have given up, Tyrolium learned to build through pain. That resilience became our DNA.

Meanwhile, institutions looked at us as "video game kids". We were met with diplomas, taxes and red tape. At 17, Maxime was already training classes of adults aged 30 to 40. No diploma. Just his keyboard.

Tyrolium is proof that a generation said to be asleep knows how to stand up. The one that grew up on YouTube, Minecraft and Discord. The one that creates instead of consuming. The one that will build tomorrow.

Our red lines

What we will never do.

Never

Sell your data

Never. To anyone. At any price.

Never

Host our data outside France

Our data stays on French soil. No conditions.

Never

Be acquired

Tyrolium is not for sale. Three serious offers have already been turned down since 2017.

Never

Raise funds

Fully self-funded. Independence is the only way to credibly promise we will compete with the giants.

Never

Judge by a diploma

Passion, talent and resilience come before the résumé.

Never

Impose crunch

Small teams, well-thought-out projects, and respect for those who create.

And this is not marketing.

Our two data commitments - zero resale, hosting in France - are written in black and white in our contracts. If we break them, drag us to court. With our own contracts as evidence.

A word from the founder

Tyrolium is my life.

Tyrolium is not just my project. It is my life. Since I was 13, every line of code, every sleepless night, every conflict and every victory has built it. Selling it would mean disowning myself.

I have often walked into big companies badly dressed. I have taught adults twice my age. I have made mistakes, plenty of them, and I own every one - they are part of the story, and I have no wish to hide them.

I carry a simple dream: to prove that a young French person with no diploma, crushed by global competition and by their own country's rules, can succeed with nothing but a keyboard. And bring a whole generation along.

Tyrolium is a company of passionate young people who are going to shake up the world - and they know it.

"Tyrolium has only ever had one dream: to create and fund the dreams of its passionate employees."
Maxime TournierCEO & Founder of Tyrolium
