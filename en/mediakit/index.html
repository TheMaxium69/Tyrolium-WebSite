About

Tyrolium is a French tech holding company founded in 2017 by digital enthusiasts. From web development to cloud infrastructure, we support every project with expertise and passion. In parallel, we build our own ecosystem of projects: from hosting to gaming, CRM and social networks. Founded by Maxime Tournier
